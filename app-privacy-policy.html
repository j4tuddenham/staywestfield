Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Westfield Guest App, provided by Westfield in connection with your stay at Westfield, a holiday cottage in Watermillock, Cumbria, overlooking Ullswater.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Westfield Guest App handles information when you use it.

The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information about its users.

2. Information We Collect

The Westfield Guest App is designed to provide information and assistance to guests during their stay.

The app does not:

  • Collect personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or postal address.
  • Require you to create an account or log in.
  • Track your location.
  • Collect information about your contacts, photos, or other personal content.
  • Use advertising or tracking technologies to monitor your activity.
  • Sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties.

3. How the App Works

The app provides information and resources to help guests make the most of their stay at Westfield.

Any information you access through the app is provided for your convenience.

The app does not maintain a personal profile of you or associate your use of the app with your identity.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may contain links to external websites or services, such as local attractions, restaurants, activities, or other useful resources.

If you follow these links, you will leave the app and access a third-party website or service.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.

5. Data Storage and Security

The app does not maintain a database of users or store personal information about guests.

We do not use the app to create user accounts or retain personal data.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that the app operates as intended and does not unnecessarily access personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Westfield Guest App is intended to provide useful information to guests of all ages.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

As the app does not collect personal information, it does not maintain personal data relating to children.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As the app does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal information held by the app for you to access, amend, or delete.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on the website where this Privacy Policy is hosted, with the revised date shown at the top of the policy.
